Limits and names

What the status page judges by. Each PLC input can be named, scaled (value = raw × scale + offset) and given rules; a rule raises its level when the value is at or above, above or below a number, or changes by at least an amount within a time. A rule can apply in one mode only. Saving writes status/limits.json on odin and appends the whole set, with the time, to status/limits_log.jsonl: what was in force when stays on record. Levels: warn = little warning (yellow), bad = big warning (red), critical = massive warning (flashing).
